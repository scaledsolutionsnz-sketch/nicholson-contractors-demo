Privacy Policy

Placeholder content for this demo site. A final policy will be supplied by Nicholson Contractors.

1. What we collect

When you contact us by phone or email we collect the details you choose to share, such as your name, contact information and the location and nature of the work you are asking about.

2. How we use it

Your details are used to respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, carry out work and invoice for it. We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing.

3. Storage

Contact details and job records are kept only as long as needed for the work and our record-keeping obligations, and are stored securely.

4. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask what personal information we hold about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Get in touch and we will sort it.

5. Website

This website does not use contact forms and does not set tracking cookies of its own. The embedded Google Map is provided by Google and is subject to Google's own privacy policy.
